How it behaves

  • Scrolling the text swaps the pinned view: a 3D design, the layer stack, a map, a chart. Each is a prepared run, so the page loads fast and never runs evolution itself.
  • On a phone the view sits above the text and changes per section. This is also what a phone gets at /workbench: the story view, not a cut-down workbench.
  • Motion only to show change, 150 to 250 ms; reduced motion turns it off.
  • Typography and the warm off-white ground match raghavkohli.xyz.

What the copy is

  • Grey bars stand for your copy. Each section has a short working title and one line of what it covers; the paragraphs are yours (marked COPY in the code).
  • Section 8 carries the A4 results once they exist; until then it says what is being tested.
