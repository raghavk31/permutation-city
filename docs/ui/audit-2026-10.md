Every step walked in the production build (headless Edge, 1440 x 900, then 1024 x 720) with a
short run: Eastern Waterfront on 23 October, Guwahati and Bengaluru on 25 October. No console
errors on any site.

list spread down the Landform rail; Massing opened on a tiny edge block; "lowest" family name.

25 October:
- **The run time estimate was about three times too short** (Guwahati: "about 18 s", measured
  61 s). It assumed the workers scale evenly; it now uses the whole-run timings, 0.35 ms per
  design cell shared over the workers.

  list of more than 8 is folded under "41 ridge streets, 41 on".

  (Guwahati). Crossings next to each other are one ring.

- **Design names like "G0.08"** in "Bred from" and in titles: now "generation 0, no. 8".
- **The evolution diagram said "after 15 generations"** whatever the run: now "generations later".
