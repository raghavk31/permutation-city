## Gandhi Ashram Comprehensive Area Improvement Scheme (GACAIS)

Buildings owned by trusts related to Gandhi Ashram's work are to be conserved and kept in
their original state; renewal or rebuilding is reviewed by the competent authority. Slums
in the area are to be rehabilitated within it, on government, municipal or trust land.
Most of the scheme area is treated as residential zone I. For plots on Ashram Road between
Wadaj Circle and Subhash Circle owned by the government, the trusts or AMC, only
residential and institutional uses (education, culture, health, tourist support such as
hotels, restaurants and craft showrooms) are allowed, with a maximum height of 10 m, light
exterior colours matching the Ashram, and tree planting in open space.

## Sabarmati Riverfront

On the riverfront, the special regulations of the Sabarmati Riverfront Development
Corporation (SRFDCL) on ground coverage, margins, height and use prevail over the general
ones.

## Restricted zones along rivers and ponds

Zone RZ1 allows only structures that control floods or prevent pollution of the river,
pond or lake and the land near it. RZ2 also allows ground-floor-only construction on up to
5 per cent of a building unit, if it preserves the natural scenery and landscape. RZ3
allows no development.

## Using these notes in Permutation City

On the Wadaj site, land along Ashram Road near the Ashram should carry a 10 m cap (about
3 storeys), and the Ashram's own buildings are retained, as the Sem 06 studio retained
them. Land along the river and the swale behaves like RZ1 or RZ2: kept for water and
landscape, which the site's forbidden and constrained rules already do.
