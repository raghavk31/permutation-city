## Base and maximum FSI by zone (AUDA)

Ahmedabad's municipal and urban development areas are Category D1 (AUDA). Each use zone
has a base (free) FSI and a chargeable FSI bought at 40 per cent of the jantri (government
land) rate. The maximum is their sum:

- Core walled city and gamtal: 2.0 (no chargeable FSI)
- Gamtal extension: 1.2 + 0.6 = 1.8
- Residential zone I (R1): 1.8 + 0.9 = 2.7
- Residential zone II (R2): 1.2 + 0.6 = 1.8
- Residential zone III (R3): 0.3 (1.2 for some educational uses)
- Commercial zone (C2): 1.8 + 0.9 = 2.7
- Knowledge and institutional zone: 1.8
- Residential affordable housing (RAH1): 1.8 + 0.9 = 2.7
- Recreation, gardens and open space: 0.15
- Central business district (C5): 1.8 + 3.6 = 5.4
- Transit oriented zone (SPD5): base zone FSI, up to 4.0 for TOZ uses
- Gandhi Ashram special area (SPD4): as applicable under its own scheme (see the Gandhi
  Ashram note)
- Sabarmati Riverfront (SPD2): as per the SRFDCL master plan

## Using these numbers in Permutation City

The Gandhi Ashram scheme treats most of its area as residential zone I. The zoning of the
rest of the Wadaj site isn't in this document (check the AUDA development plan). If it is
R1 too, a floor-area target of about FSI 1.8 (base) to 2.7 (with chargeable FSI) is the
regulatory envelope.
Land along a transit corridor may go to 4.0. The Sem 06 studio's 2050 target of
38,91,900 m² on about 1.38 km² of developable land is an FSI of about 2.8, slightly over
the R1 maximum.
