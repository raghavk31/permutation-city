## Which rules come from here

The RMP 2015 zoning regulations (2007) came after these bye-laws and set FAR, coverage and
setbacks; where they differ, this project uses the RMP (`bengaluru-rmp2015-far-setbacks`).
The bye-laws' own FAR table (Table 6, FAR 0.75-2.0) is older and not used. Bengaluru's civic
body became the Greater Bengaluru Authority in 2025; no newer bye-laws were found, so these
are taken as the ones in force (**assumed**).

## High-rise buildings and fire access

- **High-rise:** ground plus four or more floors (2.46), so five storeys and up. (The RMP's
  definition is 24 m and up; this project flags both.)
- High-rise sites at least **21 m** deep and wide (9.3.2), on roads at least **12 m** wide
  (9.3.3).
- Parking may use a high-rise building's setback only after **6 m all round** is left clear
  for fire tenders (16.0 e).

## Spacing

Two or more buildings on one site are treated as one for setbacks and stand at least half the
height of the taller apart (Table 4-6, note 12; the same as RMP 3.6).

## Daylight depth and rooms

- "No portion of a room shall be assumed to be lighted if it is more than 7.5 m from the
  opening" (20.11 (3)). Openings at least 1/6 of a residential room's floor (1/5 for offices,
  schools and hospitals).
- Habitable rooms at least 8 m² and 2.75 m high (20.1).

## Using these numbers in Permutation City

- **Depth:** 7.5 m from a window is the most a lit floor reaches, so a wing lit from both
  sides is at most 15 m deep, plus nothing between (a double-loaded corridor needs its own
  light). This is the legal ceiling for the depth rule in section 7 of the built form brief;
  the design defaults sit under it.
- **Five storeys and up** need a 21 m site, a 12 m road and 6 m clear all round: the four-
  storey default (Pattern 21) is also where the bye-laws' high-rise rules begin.
- **Floor to floor:** 2.75 m clear plus a slab gives about 3.0-3.2 m; the engine keeps
  3.2 m (assumed) and 4.5 m for an active ground floor (assumed).
