## FSI in the Island City (residential and commercial)

Table 12 sets floor space index (FSI) by road width for residential and commercial zones
in the Island City. Zonal (basic) FSI is 1.33 everywhere. On top of it come additional FSI
on payment of a premium and admissible TDR (transferable development rights). The
permissible totals are:

- road up to 9 m: 1.33
- more than 9 m up to 12.2 m: 2.0
- more than 12.2 m up to 18.3 m: 2.4
- more than 18.3 m up to 30 m: 2.7
- more than 30 m: 3.0

The Eastern Waterfront and the docks lie in the Island City.

## FSI in the suburbs, and industrial zones

In the remaining suburbs and extended suburbs, zonal FSI is 1.0 and the permissible total
rises with road width: 1.0 (up to 9 m), 2.0 (9 to 12.2 m), 2.2 (12.2 to 18.3 m), 2.4
(18.3 to 30 m) and 2.5 (over 30 m). Some areas (parts of N and M wards near BARC) are
capped at 0.75, and CRZ-affected villages such as Akse, Marve, Erangal, Gorai and Manori
at 0.5. Industrial zones in the Island City and the suburbs have zonal FSI 1.0, with up
to 2.0 using TDR for uses allowed only in the industrial zone.

## How FSI is counted

Permissible FSI is reckoned on the gross plot area, excluding land under DP roads,
regular-line road widening, DP reservations, and land handed over for amenity plots and
inclusive housing (Regulations 14 to 17). Recreational open space required under
Regulation 27 is not deducted when computing FSI. Fungible compensatory area is allowed
over and above the permissible FSI under Regulation 31(3). Where several regulations
combine on one plot, the total may not exceed the FSI limit of the applicable regulations
(Reg. 30(A)(12); the wording does not say which limit governs when they differ).

## Using these numbers in Permutation City

The engine's floor-area target is an FSI (FAR) over the design ground. For a large site
an FSI of 2.0 to 3.0 is the regulatory envelope in the Island City, depending on road
widths. The design ground excludes forbidden and retained land, which roughly matches the
gross-plot rule. Port Trust land on the eastern waterfront may be planned by its own
authority; check which regulations apply before treating these figures as binding.
