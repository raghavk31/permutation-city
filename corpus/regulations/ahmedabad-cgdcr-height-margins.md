## Height by road width

Building height follows the width of the road a plot abuts (Table 6.23). For D1
(except Gandhinagar):

- road under 9 m: 10 m
- 9 m to under 12 m: 16.5 m
- 12 m to under 18 m: 30 m
- 18 m to under 36 m: 45 m
- 36 m and above: 70 m (as read from a poorly extracted column; check)

At about 3.2 m a storey, 16.5 m is 5 storeys, 30 m about 9, 45 m about 14 and 70 m about 21.

## Road-side margins

Road-side margins for D1 rise with road width (Table 6.24): 2.5 m up to 9 m, 3.0 m up to
15 m, 4.5 m for 15 to 18 m, 6.0 m for 18 to 30 m, 7.5 m for 30 to 45 m and 9.0 m above
that. Special buildings keep 12 m. Where the height-based margin of Table 6.26 is larger,
it applies.

## Space between buildings

Between two buildings in one plot (Table 6.25, D1): 4.5 m up to 16.5 m high, 6 m up to
25 m, 9 m up to 45 m and 12 m up to 70 m. Buildings keep 3 m from the common plot up to
25 m high, and 6 m above.

## Using these numbers in Permutation City

On the 20 m grid, the height cap per cell is the place for these limits: most internal
streets of a neighbourhood are 9 to 12 m, which allows 16.5 m (5 storeys), and arterial
roads of 18 m or more allow up to 45 m. A brief can set a site-wide cap of about 5
storeys for a fabric of local streets, or leave higher caps near major roads to the rules.
