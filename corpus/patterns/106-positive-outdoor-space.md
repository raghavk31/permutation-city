## Pattern 106: Positive Outdoor Space

Intent (paraphrased): outdoor spaces should have a definite shape, partly enclosed by
buildings, so they are places people use, not leftovers between buildings. A space is
positive when it is enclosed enough to feel convex.

Use in Permutation City: the positive-space metric of Phase 6 (area of each open patch
over the area of its convex hull, weighted by area), one of the fifteen properties from
The Nature of Order as well. Until then the brief records it.
