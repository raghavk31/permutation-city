## Pattern 21: Four-Story Limit

Intent (paraphrased): in most of a town, keep most buildings at four storeys or fewer, so
people stay in touch with the street and the ground. Some buildings can be taller, but
they should be the exception.

Use in Permutation City: a constraint. When switched on, the brief caps heights at about 4
storeys over most of the design ground (the brief's height limit). Taller exceptions are
left to the rules (for example near transit), not to the cap. The number 4 is the book's;
the allowed range of 3 to 5 is ours. Unchecked against the book: treat as assumed.
