## Pattern 60: Accessible Green

Intent (paraphrased): people need open green within a few minutes' walk of where they
live and work, and the green has to be big enough to feel like a place of its own, not a
strip. The book gives a walking time of about three minutes and a minimum size; both are
to be checked against the book before use.

Use in Permutation City: a metric from Phase 6 (walking distance from building cells to a
green patch above a minimum size). Until then, a brief can raise the lower end of the green
share so every design keeps more green. Numbers unchecked: treat as assumed.
