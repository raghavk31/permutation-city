## Pattern 107: Wings of Light

Intent (paraphrased): buildings should be made of long, narrow wings so every room gets
daylight, instead of deep floor plates with dark interiors. The book gives a maximum wing
width; to be checked against the book.

Use in Permutation City: a prior for building-scale massing (narrow wings, 9 to 12 m until checked, in
the courtyard, L, U and slab typologies). It cannot apply on the 20 m site grid, so the
brief records it and may favour courtyard, L and U typologies. Numbers unchecked.
