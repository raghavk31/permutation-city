## Pattern 61: Small Public Squares

Intent (paraphrased): public squares feel alive when they are small, so that people in
them are close enough to see each other; large plazas feel empty. The book gives a range
of widths, roughly 15 to 20 metres; to be checked against the book.

Use in Permutation City: a metric in Phase 6 (the size of open-space patches that front
buildings on several sides). Until then the brief records it. Numbers unchecked: treat as
assumed.
