## Pattern 159: Light on Two Sides of Every Room

Intent (paraphrased): rooms lit from two sides are calmer and more pleasant to be in than
rooms lit from one, so plans should be shallow enough that most rooms have windows on two
sides.

Use in Permutation City: a prior for Phase 7 massing (shallow plans, dual-aspect wings).
On the site grid it is recorded only, and may favour slab and L typologies over deep
blocks.
