## The 2050 scenario: how much to build

The site is 1.45 km² in central north Ahmedabad, by the Sabarmati, with 1.38 km² to
develop. The studio projected the population and the built-up area it needs:

- 2025: 65,873 people; 16,46,825 m² built up; 5,92,857 m² of open space; about 4 floors
- 2035: 92,921 people; 23,23,025 m² built up; 8,36,289 m² of open space; about 7 floors
- 2050: 1,55,676 people; 38,91,900 m² built up; 14,01,084 m² of open space

In 2050 the open space needed exceeds the ground available, so the studio put part of it
on roofs (5,64,795 m² of roof open space), which brought the height down to about 12
floors on average (the figure for keeping all open space on the ground extracts as "151"
from the PDF; it reads as about 15). 38,91,900 m² is the floor-area target of the
`ahmedabad-wadaj` site (389 ha).

## Retained areas

Four places were kept and never built on: the sewage treatment plant (20,081 m²), Gandhi
Ashram (21,256 m²), the bioswale (15,000 m²) and Kalam Kush (6,762 m²), 6.3 ha in all.

## Density from attractors

Density came from attractors with radii that change over time. High density was pulled
towards bus and metro stops, junctions and the transit-oriented edge (100 to 150 m in 2025,
growing to 150 to 250 m by 2050). Low density was kept near the swale and green patch, the
Ashram, the treatment plant and Kalam Kush (100 to 150 m in 2025, shrinking to 50 to 100 m
by 2050). The built mix moved from 45/31/24 per cent low/medium/high in 2025 to 33/24/43 in
2050.

## Land use

The studio's land use split was about 85 per cent residential, 25 per cent commercial
(overlapping in mixed use) and 35 per cent open and green space. High-density housing sits
by transit and major nodes, commercial along the primary network and stops, and green
space along the swale and spread through the neighbourhood.

## Using this in Permutation City

This is the brief the Ahmedabad site was built from. It supports a floor-area target of
389 ha, a green share of 30 to 40 per cent, core attractors near transit and low density
near the Ashram, swale and treatment plant, and growing in steps (2025, 2035, 2050).
